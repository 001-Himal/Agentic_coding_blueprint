# accessibility.md

Semantic HTML, keyboard nav, focus states, contrast AA, labels on inputs, alt text.

Contrast specifics: every text/bg pairing ≥ 4.5:1 (≥3:1 large text/UI components) in light AND dark themes, all states (hover/focus/disabled), and over images/overlays — verify with a checker, not eyeballing. No color-only meaning.
See also `rules/accessibility.md` (rule) and `verification/accessibility.md` (check).
